# Mascot assets

- `idle.webp`: the approved canonical pug host, generated with `fal-ai/nano-banana-2` (text to image) from a prompt asking for a simple flat 2D vector pug with bold outlines, a plain red bow tie, no blush and no whiskers on a lavender background. Chosen by the product owner from three candidates ("a") on 2026-10-11. The web copy has its flat lavender ground cut out and its baked-in ground shadow recoloured to a neutral translucent shadow with `scripts/mascot-cutout.py`, applied to `packages/infra/assets/pug-mascot.webp` (the unmodified original, which the hint generator uses as its reference).
