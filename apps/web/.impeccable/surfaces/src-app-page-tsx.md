# Surface brief: SnapHunt app shell and home (redesign)

Scope: the whole web app's visual system (theme, type, controls) and the home screen. This replaces the earlier lavender and violet world, which read as a kids' app. Mode: Operate (players complete a task on a phone, outdoors). Audience and job: 2-6 friends of any age, each on one phone, creating or joining a room and playing a 10-30 minute round. Constraints: bright sunlight, one-handed, text always accompanies audio, reduced motion respected. Memorable moment: the confident green button that physically presses down, and the pug host standing right on the white page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: SnapHunt is a polished, grown-up-friendly game UI in the manner of Duolingo: clean white, one confident green action per screen, tactile pressed-edge controls and a pug host; it refuses the childish pastel, bubbly-font look and the grey SaaS card grid.

OWN-WORLD: A light theme on a clean white ground. Named roles: Leaf (confident green, the primary action, white text at 4.5:1 or better), Line (light neutral borders and edges), Ink (dark green-black text), Sun (points and streak-like highlights), Bow (the bow-tie red, danger and low time), Sky (blue for links and information), Fawn (the pug's coat, ready and host states). Rubik at heavy weights for headings and buttons, Nunito for body. Controls have a 2px line border with a 4px solid darker bottom edge that collapses on press; corners are about 14-16px, not pill or bubble; button labels are uppercase with light tracking. The pug stands directly on the page on a soft neutral ground shadow, with no tile behind it.

STORY: A player opens the page and knows within a second what to do next. It feels like a polished modern app that adults and kids are equally comfortable using; there is one primary action per screen and the pug reacts to what just happened.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone portrait, 390 x 844. SnapHunt wordmark in Rubik and Leaf at the top. The pug host fills the middle, about 40% of the height, on white. Below it, two full-width stacked chunky buttons about 56px tall: "CREATE A ROOM" in Leaf (primary) and "JOIN WITH A CODE" in white with a Line border (secondary). One small line of copy beneath: "2-6 friends. One phone each." On wider screens the same column stays centered at a phone-like width.

FORM: Pinned by the brief (Duolingo polish for all ages, replacing the earlier Kahoot-leaning direction); not rolled with concept-seed, so there is no seed key. Duolingo lends the white ground, one green action, the bordered pressed-edge controls and encouraging tone; it is not copied: the palette, type, mascot and uppercase labels are SnapHunt's own.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
