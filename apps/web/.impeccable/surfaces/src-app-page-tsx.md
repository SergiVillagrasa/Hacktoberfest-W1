# Surface brief: SnapHunt app shell and home

Scope: the whole web app's visual system (theme, type, controls) and the home screen. Mode: Operate (players complete a task on a phone, outdoors). Audience and job: 2-6 friends, each on one phone, creating or joining a room and playing a 10-30 minute round. Constraints: bright sunlight, one-handed, text always accompanies audio, reduced motion respected. Memorable moment: the chunky button that physically presses down, and the pug host standing on the home screen.

## Direction contract

THESIS: SnapHunt is a tactile game controller for the outdoors, one huge pressable action per screen and a pug host beside it; it refuses the grey-and-blue SaaS card grid and the cream-and-serif "friendly" default.

OWN-WORLD: A full-palette light theme tinted from the pug art. Named roles: Grape (deep violet, brand and primary, white text), Lilac (tinted page ground behind everything), Fawn (the pug's coat, secondary and ready states), Bow (the bow-tie red, danger and low time), Mint (success), Sun (points and score highlights), Ink (violet-black text). Fredoka for display, buttons and numbers, Nunito for body, both at heavy weights. Components are chunky and rounded: buttons and cards carry a solid darker bottom edge that collapses on press, borders are thick and tinted, corners are 16-24px, tap targets are at least 56px.

STORY: A player opens the page and knows within a second what to do next. The game feels playful and kind, never confusing; there is one primary action per screen and the pug reacts to what just happened.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone portrait, 390 x 844. SnapHunt wordmark in Fredoka and Grape at the top. The pug host fills the middle, about 40% of the height, on the lilac ground. Below it, two full-width stacked chunky buttons: "Create a room" in Grape (primary) and "Join with a code" in Fawn (secondary), 64px tall. One small line of copy beneath: "2-6 friends. One phone each." On wider screens the same column stays centered at a phone-like width.

FORM: Pinned by the brief (Kahoot and Duolingo feel with a 2D pug host); not rolled with concept-seed, so there is no seed key. Kahoot lends the bold color blocks, Duolingo the tactile pressed-edge buttons and encouragement.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
